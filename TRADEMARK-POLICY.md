The names **STAMM** and **FermOps**, the STAMM logo and the associated visual
identity (the "Marks") identify the software framework developed at TBI/INRAE.
The open-source licenses that cover the STAMM source code grant rights over the
*code*; they grant **no rights over the Marks**. This policy explains what you
may and may not do with them.

## You may, without asking

* Use the Marks to refer truthfully to the software: "built with STAMM",
  "compatible with the STAMM Model Registry", "a FermOps dashboard".
* Use the Marks in academic publications, teaching material, talks and posters
  that describe or evaluate STAMM, with a citation to the STAMM paper.
* Redistribute unmodified copies of STAMM modules under their names.

## You may not, without written permission

* Use STAMM, FermOps or a confusingly similar name as the name of a product,
  service, company, domain name or package that is not the original software
  (e.g. "STAMM Pro", "StammCloud", "FermOps Enterprise", `pip install stamm2`).
* Present a modified version as the official STAMM or FermOps. Forks must be
  renamed and must state clearly that they are derived from STAMM.
* Use the logo or visual identity in a way that suggests endorsement,
  partnership or certification by TBI, INRAE or the STAMM authors.
* Register any of the Marks, or a variation of them, as a trademark, company
  name or domain name.
